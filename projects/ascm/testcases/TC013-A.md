# [ASC-157]
Case 3 - B2B REST — Remaining Tickets Must Expire When Contract Ends (No Renewal)

> **Override of TC013:** Uses the original ASC-157 Case 3 dates (`end_date = 12/29`, within the month). TC013 used rewritten dates from ASC-247 (`end_date = 03/01`, day 1 of next month) which accidentally triggered the lookahead path. This override aligns with the original spec.

[Story]
When a B2B student's contract expires and is not renewed, the student enters REST status. All remaining tickets at the contract end date must be counted as expired in the final charge's monthly report row. There is no Charge 2 since the contract was not renewed.

[Precondition]
* **Student [student_id]** has the following charges (plan: 8 lessons/month, B2B):

| order_no | charge_id | start_date | end_date | ticket_expiry | usage_notes |
|----------|-----------|-----------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| [order_no] | [charge_1_id] | 2025-11-30 | 2025-12-29 | 2026-01-29 | Charge 1. 1 lesson on 11/30, 5 lessons 12/1–12/29. Contract expires 12/29. No renewal → REST on 12/30. Remaining 2 tickets expire. |

* No Charge 2 (contract not renewed)

[Steps]
1. Student takes 1 lesson on 11/30 (Charge 1)
2. Student takes 5 lessons between 12/1–12/29 (Charge 1, total taken=6, remaining=2)
3. Contract expires on 12/29. Student does not renew → enters REST status on 12/30
4. No Charge 2 is issued
5. Generate the November monthly report (target_ym=202511) via the 202512 batch
6. Generate the December monthly report (target_ym=202512) via the 202601 batch

[Expected]

**① November Report: 202511_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20251201).csv**

```csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202511,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],8,0,1,0,7,1320
```

*Charge 1 (Nov): taken=1 (lesson on 11/30), remaining=7, paid_price=1,320 (¥1,320 × 1).*

**② December Report: 202512_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260101).csv**

```csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202512,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],8,7,5,2,0,9240
```

*Charge 1 (Dec): carried_over=7 (from Nov remaining), taken=5, expired=2 (remaining 2 tickets expire when contract ends on 12/29 — B2B REST, no renewal), remaining=0, paid_price=9,240 (¥1,320 × (5+2)).*

No Charge 2 row — contract was not renewed.

[Acceptance Criteria]
- When a B2B contract expires without renewal, all remaining tickets must be counted as expired in the month where `end_date` falls.
- No carry-over should occur after contract expiry under REST status.
- Expiry appears in December (the month `end_date = 12/29` falls in) — NOT pulled back to November.

[Notes]
- This test case uses the original ASC-157 Case 3 dates where `end_date = 12/29` (within December).
- The lookahead condition is NOT triggered because `end_date` does not exceed `LAST_DAY(month_start)`.
- Expiry fires via `is_last_charge_month` (end_date month = December = current month).
- Source: Original ASC-157 ticket, Case 3: REST.
