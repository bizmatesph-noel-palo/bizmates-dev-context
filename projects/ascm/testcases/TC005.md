[ASC-151]
Case 8b - Monthly-15 Plan → B2B Refund Processing (Refund Executed in Following Month)

[Story]
When a student on a monthly-15 B2C plan transitions to B2B mid-month, remaining tickets at the B2B start date are settled and a refund charge is issued. When the refund is executed in the month following the B2B start, the refund row must appear in the following month's CSV — not the B2B start month. The new B2B Charge 2 must appear in the B2B start month's CSV even if no lessons were taken.

How to Verify:
[Precondition] Student [student_id] has the following charges (B2C plan: 15 lessons/month at ¥980/ticket = ¥14,700/month):

order_no,charge_id,type,start_date,end_date,usage_notes
[order_no],[charge_1_id],B2C,2026-01-01,2026-01-31,"Charge 1. 10 lessons taken 1/1–1/24. B2B starts 1/25 → 5 tickets remaining at settlement."
[order_no],[charge_2_id],B2B,2026-01-25,2026-02-24,"Charge 2. B2B new charge. No lessons taken through end of January."
[order_no],[refund_charge_id],Refund,2026-02-01,2026-02-01,"Refund executed in February (following month). 5 tickets × ¥980 = ¥4,900."

[Steps]
Student takes 10 lessons between 1/1–1/24 (Charge 1, 5 tickets remaining)
Admin registers B2B contract on 1/20 (B2B start: 1/25) → ticket settlement scheduled
B2B starts 1/25 → 5 remaining tickets confirmed; refund = 5 × ¥980 = ¥4,900
Refund is executed on 2/1 (following month — February)
Generate the January monthly report (target_ym=202601) via the 202602 batch
Generate the February monthly report (target_ym=202602) via the 202603 batch

[Expected]

File1: 202601_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260201).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],15,0,10,5,0,14700
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_2_id],8,0,0,0,8,0

Charge 1 (normal):
taken=10, expired=5 (remaining tickets voided at B2B start 1/25), remaining=0, paid_price=14700 (¥980 × (10+5)).

Charge 2 (B2B new):
contract starts 1/25, no lessons taken through end of January.
Must appear in CSV.
remaining=8, paid_price=0.

Refund charge does NOT appear in this CSV — it is attributed to the month of execution (February).

File2: 202602_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260301).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202602,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[refund_charge_id],,,,,,,-4900

Refund charge:
paid_price=-4900 (¥980 × 5 negative).
total, carried_over, taken, expired, remaining left blank.

Refund target_ym=202602 because execution happened in February.
