# [ASC-254]
First Charge Expiration Bug — Remaining Tickets Prematurely Expired

[Story]
Even though a charge is an active contract item within an ongoing order string, its remaining tickets are being incorrectly processed as "Expired-tickets" at the end of the monthly boundary calculation. Since the contract order is continuous and has valid successor records, these remaining tickets must not be prematurely flushed to expired status.

[Precondition]
* Both cases exist on an active monthly plan (8 lessons / month).
* Ongoing consecutive contracts exist in the database with identical `order_no` configurations.
