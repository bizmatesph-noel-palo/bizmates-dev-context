Case 1 - Carry-Over Tickets — 60-Day Validity (Same order_no Required)

[Story]
When a student is on a standard monthly plan (except FLP 15 lessons/month), unused tickets carry a 60-day validity window as long as subsequent charges remain under the same `order_no`. If a new charge cycle (Charge 2) begins within the target month but has 0 lessons taken, the system must still generate and display its corresponding row in the monthly CSV with `remaining=8` and `paid_price=0`. The current system fails to display these zero-activity rows, violating the baseline reporting rules.

[Precondition]
* **Plan:** 8 lessons/month plan (Unit price calculation basis: ¥1,320 per lesson)
* **Charge 1 Timeline:** 11/30 ~ 12/29 (Ticket expiry: 1/29 based on 60-day validity window)
* **Charge 2 Timeline:** Starts 12/30 under the **same order_no**
* **FLP Exception Rule:** This standard 60-day carry-over extension does NOT apply to FLP (15 lessons / month) plans, which expire strictly at the contract end date.

[Steps]
1. Initialize Charge 1 on 11/30. Record 1 lesson on 11/30 and 5 lessons between 12/1 ~ 12/29.
2. Trigger Charge 2 on 12/30 under the same `order_no` but record 0 usage.
3. Run report generation sequences for cycles 202511, 202512, and 202601.
