[ASC-151]
Case 9a - Cooling-Off — Lessons Taken, Same-Month Execution

[Story]
When a student executes a cooling-off within 8 days of plan start and has already taken lessons, the normal charge row must appear with taken and expired values, plus a separate CF refund charge row with a negative paid_price. Both rows must appear in the same month's CSV when the cooling-off is executed in the same month as the plan start.

How to Verify:
[Precondition] Student [student_id] has the following charges (plan: 15 lessons/month at ¥980/ticket = ¥14,700/month):
order_no,charge_id,type,start_date,end_date,usage_notes
[order_no],[charge_1_id],B2C,2026-01-01,2026-01-31,"Charge 1. 10 lessons taken 1/1–1/7. Cooling-off executed 1/8 (within 8 days). 5 remaining tickets expired."
[order_no],[cf_refund_id],CF Refund,2026-01-08,2026-01-08,"CF refund. 5 remaining tickets × ¥980 = ¥4,900 negative. Full plan amount ¥14,700 negative."

[Steps]
Student takes 10 lessons between 1/1–1/7 (5 tickets remaining)
Cooling-off is executed on 1/8 (within 8 days of plan start) → remaining 5 tickets become expired
Generate the January monthly report (target_ym=202601) via the 202602 batch

[Expected]

File1: 202601_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260201).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],15,0,10,5,0,14700
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[cf_refund_id],,,,,,,-4900

Charge 1 (normal):
taken=10, expired=5 (remaining tickets voided by cooling-off on 1/8), remaining=0, paid_price=14700 (¥980 × (10+5)).

CF refund charge:
paid_price=-4900 (¥980 × 5 negative). total, carried_over, taken, expired, remaining left blank.

Both rows appear in target_ym=202601 because cooling-off was executed in January.
