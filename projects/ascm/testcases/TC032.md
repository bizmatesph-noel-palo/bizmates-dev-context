# [ASC-283]
Monthly Rate Calculation — Incorrect Records (Duplicates, Over-Count, Wrong Carry-Over, Missing Records)

[Story]
Four patterns of incorrect data were identified in `log_monthly_rate_calculation` during data adjustment.
Each pattern maps to a distinct root cause fixed in child tickets ASC-285, ASC-286, and ASC-287.

---

## Pattern C — Bizmates: Lessons Over-Counted Across Months (Fixed by ASC-287)

### [Precondition - Pattern C]

* **Service:** Bizmates
* **Plan:** 15 lessons/month (product_id 29)
* **Charge:** Starts April 3 – May 2
* **Lessons:** 14 lessons taken in April (all before May 1), 1 lesson on May 1
* **Total actual lessons taken:** 15 (= plan limit)

### [Test Data - Pattern C]

| charge_id | student_id | sp_start | sp_end | paid_price | Lessons Apr 3-30 | Lessons May 1 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|--------|-----------|------------------|---------------|
| 3028086 | 67915 | 2026-04-03 | 2026-05-02 | 14107 | 14 | 1 |

### [Expected - Pattern C]

```csv
target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202604,67915,3028086,15,0,14,0,1,13167
202605,67915,3028086,15,1,1,0,0,940
```

*Note: April counts only lessons with lesson_datetime < 2026-05-01 (14 lessons). 1 remaining carries to May. May counts the May 1 lesson. Sum = 14+1 = 15 = plan limit. paid_price = (14107/15) × taken per month.*

### [Before Fix - Pattern C (current incorrect output)]

```csv
target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202604,67915,3028086,15,0,15,0,0,14107
202605,67915,3028086,15,1,1,0,0,940
```

*Bug: April counts 15 (includes May 1 via lookahead). May also counts 1. Sum = 16 > 15. Total paid = 14107+940 = 15047 > actual 14107.*

### [Validation - Pattern C]

1. Run April batch against Bizmates
2. Query: `SELECT * FROM log_monthly_rate_calculation WHERE charge_id = 3028086 ORDER BY target_ym`
3. Confirm April `number_of_lessons_taken = 14` (not 15)
4. Confirm May `number_of_lessons_taken = 1`, `number_of_carried_over_lessons = 1`
5. Confirm sum of lessons_taken across months ≤ total (15)
6. Confirm lessons on April 30 (e.g., lesson_datetime = 2026-04-30 23:30) are still counted in April

## Pattern D — Bizmates: Wrong Carry-Over + Combined Bug (Fixed by ASC-285 + ASC-287)

### [Precondition - Pattern D]

* **Service:** Bizmates
* **Plan:** 15 lessons/month (product_id 29)
* **Charge:** Starts April 3 – May 2
* **Lessons:** 6 lessons in April (Apr 4 × 4, Apr 13 × 2), 5 lessons in May (May 1 × 2, May 2 × 3)
* **order_no:** NULL
* **Same-student successor exists** (charge 3065608, starts 2026-05-03)

### [Test Data - Pattern D]

| charge_id | student_id | sp_start | sp_end | paid_price | Lessons Apr | Lessons May 1-2 |
|-----------|-----------|----------|--------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| 3028147 | 62241 | 2026-04-03 | 2026-05-02 | 14107 | 6 | 5 |

### [Expected - Pattern D (after both ASC-285 + ASC-287)]

```csv
target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202604,62241,3028147,15,0,6,0,9,5644
202605,62241,3028147,15,9,5,4,0,8464
```

*Note: April: taken=6 (strict boundary, May lessons not counted), remaining=9 carries over (successor exists via NULL-safe check). May: carried_over=9, taken=5, expired=4. paid_price = (14107/15) × (taken+expired) per month. Sum = 5644+8464 = 14108 ≈ 14107 ✓*

### [Before Fix - Pattern D (current incorrect output)]

```csv

202605,62241,3028147,15,9,5,4,0,8464
```

*Bug: April taken=8 (includes 2 May-1 lessons via lookahead) + expired=7 (premature expiry from NULL order). Total paid = 14107+8464 = 22571 > actual 14107.*

### [Validation - Pattern D]

1. Apply ASC-285 first, then ASC-287
2. Run April batch against Bizmates
3. Query: `SELECT * FROM log_monthly_rate_calculation WHERE charge_id = 3028147 ORDER BY target_ym`
4. Confirm April: `taken=6, expired=0, remaining=9`
5. Confirm May: `carried_over=9, taken=5, expired=4`
6. Confirm sum of paid_price = charge paid_price (14107 ± rounding)

[Acceptance Criteria]

1. Pattern A: All 4 Zipan charges must appear in `target_ym = 202604` with correct values (after ASC-286)
2. Pattern B: NULL order_no charges must not expire prematurely — remaining lessons carry over when successor exists (after ASC-285)
3. Pattern C: Sum of `number_of_lessons_taken` across months must not exceed `total` / lesson_volume (after ASC-287)
4. Pattern D: Combined fix — April must show strict-boundary lesson count + correct carry-over via NULL-safe successor detection (after ASC-285 + ASC-287)
5. Sum of `paid_price` across all months for a single charge must equal the charge's actual `paid_price` (± ¥1 rounding)
6. No regression on existing test cases (TC014-TC030)
7. No regression on charges with non-NULL order_no
8. Daily pipeline (`log_daily_rate_calculation`) must remain unaffected
