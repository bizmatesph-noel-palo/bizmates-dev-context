[ASC-247]
ASC-ZEPHYR-SUMMARY Monthly Plan Test Matrix — Calculation Validation for B2B/B2C Varied Plans

[Story]
This test matrix provides the baseline for validating the `paid_price` calculation logic across different student types (B2E, B2C), payment methods, and specialized plans (Campaign, Loyal AND/OR, Coaching). The system must ensure that `paid_price` strictly follows the formula: **(Taken + Expired) × Unit Price**, while maintaining high precision for fractional unit prices.

[Precondition]
All tests assume a standard monthly reporting cycle. Unit prices are derived from `Plan Price ÷ Total (Plan Limit)`.

[Acceptance Criteria]

* Carried-over tickets must be accounted for in the "Total" or "Remaining" columns but only **Taken + Expired** should trigger revenue in the current `paid_price`.
* Bank transfer plans must dynamically calculate the unit price based on the Admin-inserted price.
