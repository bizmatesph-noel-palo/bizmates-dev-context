[ASC-203]
Incorrect sales amount in CalculationSummary.csv — not reflecting actual ticket consumption

[Story]
The 支払金額 (sales amount) in CalculationSummary.csv is incorrectly calculated. It should reflect the actual consumption of tickets (taken + expired) multiplied by the unit price, aggregated for all students under the same order number. Currently, the system is reporting inflated values that do not match the sum of lesson activity.

[Precondition]
Order/Dept Info: order_no 10022381 / department_id 11110
Pricing: unit_price = 1,254円

Consumption Logic:
1月 (January): 7 taken + 0 expired = 7 total. Expected: 1,254 × 7 = 8,778
2月 (February): 7 taken + 10 expired = 17 total. Expected: 1,254 × 17 = 21,318

[Steps]
Identify all students under order_no 10022381.
Calculate the sum of (taken + expired) tickets for these students.
Generate the January and February CalculationSummary reports.
