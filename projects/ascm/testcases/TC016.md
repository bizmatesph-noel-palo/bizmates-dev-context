[ASC-205]
Missing Carried Over Charge ID — Only Current Charge ID Recorded

[Story]
When a student has multiple valid charge IDs within a reporting period (e.g., Charge A carried over and Charge B active), the monthly report fails to display the carried-over record.
In this case, Charge 12235 (Charge A) is missing from the output, while only Charge 12520 (Charge B) is recorded. This results in missing data for lessons actually taken during the period.

[Precondition]
Student (ZS0000001964) has 2 charge IDs:
Charge A (12235): Term 2026/01/08 – 2026/02/07 (Carried over into Feb)
Charge B (12520): Term 2026/02/08 – 2026/03/07

Usage History:
2 tickets used during Feb: 2026/02/03 14:00 and 2026/02/06 14:00.
Both lessons belong to Charge A (12235).

[Steps]
Generate the February monthly report (target_ym=202602) via the 202603 batch.
Search for student_id "1964".

[Expected]
**① February Data : 202602 CSV**
File: 202602_04_CalculationSummary(YYYYMMDD).csv
コンテンツ,target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
Zipan,202602,1964,12235,5,2,2,0,0,5500
Zipan,202602,1964,12520,5,0,0,0,5,0

[Actual]
**① February Data : 202602 CSV**
File: 202602_04_CalculationSummary(YYYYMMDD).csv
コンテンツ,target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
Zipan,202602,1964,12520,5,0,0,0,5,0

Note:
The first row (Charge 12235) is missing/NOT displayed.

[Comparison]
Charge 12235: Expected to appear with 2 lessons taken and 5,500 paid_price. Actual: Row is entirely missing.
Charge 12520: Appears correctly but does not reflect the total activity for the student in February.

[Acceptance Criteria]
Monthly reports must include all charge IDs that were active or had usage during the target_ym.
Carried-over charges that have lessons taken or expired within the month must not be filtered out or suppressed.
