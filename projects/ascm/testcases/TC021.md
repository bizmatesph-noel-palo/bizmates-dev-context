[ASC-232]
Zipan Monthly Report — Invalid Third Charge ID (Expired Data Leak)

[Story]
For Zipan, tickets have a maximum validity of one month. Therefore, a monthly report should never contain more than two Charge IDs per student (the carried-over charge and the current active charge). In this case, an old, unrelated Charge ID (11593) is incorrectly appearing in the February report with zero values, violating the business logic and cluttering the data.

[Precondition]
Student (ZS0000001964) has exactly 2 valid charge IDs for the period:

* **Charge A (12235):** 2026/01/08 – 2026/02/07 (Carried over)
* **Charge B (12520):** 2026/02/08 – 2026/03/07 (Current)

**Usage Context:**
* 2 tickets used on 2/3 and 2/6 (belonging to Charge A).

[Steps]
1. Generate the February monthly report (target_ym=202602) via the 202603 batch.
2. Search for student_id "1964".

[Expected]
**① February Data : 202602 CSV**
File: 202602_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(YYYYMMDD).csv
コンテンツ,target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
Zipan,202602,1964,12235,5,2,2,0,0,5500
Zipan,202602,1964,12520,5,0,0,0,5,0

**Only 2 rows should exist:**

[Actual]
**① February Data : 202602 CSV**
File: 202602_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(YYYYMMDD).csv
コンテンツ,target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
Zipan,202602,1964,11593,5,0,0,0,0,0
Zipan,202602,1964,12235,5,2,2,0,0,5500
Zipan,202602,1964,12520,5,0,0,0,5,0

**3 rows are displayed (Charge 11593 should not be here):**

[Comparison]
* **Charge 11593:** This is an old charge that is completely unrelated to the February reporting period. It appears with 0 usage/remaining but should be excluded entirely.
* **Charges 12235 & 12520:** These are correct, but the presence of the 3rd ID causes a data integrity issue.

[Acceptance Criteria]
* The Zipan monthly report must filter out any Charge IDs that do not have active validity or lesson activity within the `target_ym`.
* Ensure that "zero-activity" historical charges (like 11593) are not pulled into the `CalculationSummary.csv`.
* The maximum number of charge ID rows per student for Zipan should be limited to 2.
