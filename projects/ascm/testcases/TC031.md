# [ASC-280]
Monthly Rate Calculation — Orphaned Charges (Deleted Tickets) Missing from Log Table

[Story]
When a student's monthly plan charge has all its associated tickets deleted (typically due to B2B→B2E transition or plan change), the charge is completely absent from `log_monthly_rate_calculation`.
This happens because the Monthly Rate Calculation CTE relies on `trn_ticket` as the entry point — if no tickets exist, the charge is never processed.

The daily pipeline (`log_daily_rate_calculation`) captures these charges normally since it queries `trn_charge` directly.
This creates a discrepancy between daily and monthly summaries, affecting accounting data.

[Root Cause]
The `TicketMonths` CTE starts with `FROM trn_ticket t WHERE ticket_type = 3`.
If all tickets for a `student_product_id` have been deleted, `TicketMonths` produces 0 rows.
`MonthlyUsage` JOINs to `TicketMonths` → 0 rows.
The charge is invisible to the entire pipeline.

[Business Rules — Confirmed by Kuroda-san 2026-06-08]
- Tickets are deleted at contract renewal (= charge end_date)
- Tickets are only deleted when NONE were ever used for a booking (zero lessons taken)
- The orphaned charge is recognized in the month its end_date falls in
- If at least one ticket was used → tickets are NOT deleted → normal CTE processing

[Trigger Scenarios]
- B2B→B2E transition: unused monthly tickets from the last B2B charge are deleted at renewal
- Plan change: old plan tickets deleted when new plan activates at renewal
- Charge batch renewal with reserved payment

[Affected Plans]
All monthly plans (product_id: 16-23, 27-29).
Not limited to the 15-times plan.

### Case 1: Single Charge — Orphaned (Last and Only Charge)

#### [Precondition - Case 1]

* **Plan:** 8 lessons/month (unit price: ¥1,100 per lesson, total: ¥8,800)
* **Charge 1:** May 1 – May 31 (only charge, last charge of order)
* **Transition:** B2B→B2E reserved. All tickets for Charge 1 deleted at renewal (May 31).
* **Lessons taken:** 0

#### [Expected - Case 1]

**① May Data : 202605 CSV**

```csv
202605,[student_id],[charge_1_id],8,0,0,8,0,8800
```

*Note: end_date = May 31 → orphaned charge recognized in May. All lessons expired. paid_price = full amount.*

### Case 5: Orphaned Charge Crossing Two Months

#### [Precondition - Case 5]

* **Plan:** 8 lessons/month (unit price: ¥1,320 per lesson, total: ¥10,560 — B2B monthly-8)
* **Charge 1:** April 15 – May 14 (last B2B charge, all tickets deleted due to B2E transition at renewal May 14/15)
* **No prior charges in this order**
* **Lessons taken:** 0

#### [Expected - Case 5]

**① May Data : 202605 CSV**

```csv
target_ym,student_id,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202605,[student_id],[charge_1_id],8,0,0,8,0,10560
```

*Note: Charge starts in April but end_date = May 14. Tickets deleted at renewal (May 14/15). Orphaned charge recognized in May (the month end_date falls in). All 8 lessons expired. Full paid_price recognized in May.*

*The charge does NOT appear in April because: (a) it has no tickets so the CTE doesn't see it in April, and (b) the orphaned query filters on end_date in target month, which is May.*

[Acceptance Criteria]

1. Charges with all tickets deleted must appear in `log_monthly_rate_calculation` / `log_monthly_rate_calculation_pre` with correct values.
2. The orphaned charge query runs after the main CTE, following the same merge pattern as the refund query (ASC-276).
3. The orphaned charge is recognized in the **month its end_date falls in** (not paid_at month).
4. `number_of_lessons_taken` = 0 for all orphaned charges (tickets only deleted when zero bookings made).
5. `number_of_expired_lessons` = `lesson_volume` (full plan amount).
6. `paid_price` = full charge paid_price (entire contract value recognized in expiry month).
7. The fix must cover ALL monthly plan product IDs (Bizmates: 16-23, 27-29; Zipan: 16-18).
8. No regression on existing test cases (TC014-TC030).
9. The daily pipeline (`log_daily_rate_calculation`) must remain unaffected.
10. Charges with partial ticket deletion (some tickets remain) must continue through normal CTE processing.
