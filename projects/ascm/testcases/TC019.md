Bizmates B2B - Last Charge ID Double Counted in Monthly Reports

[Story]
Monthly report has 3 chargeIDs in Bizmates.
The final charge of an order (charge_id: 2768091) is being incorrectly carried over into the March report (February data), even though its validity should have expired on 2026/01/10.
