[ASC-151]
Case 7 - Zipan Monthly Plan — No Carryover (Ticket Validity = Contract Period)

[Story]
Zipan plans (B2C: monthly-5 only; B2B: monthly 5, 10, 15) have no ticket carryover. Ticket validity is strictly limited to the contract period. When a new contract cycle (Charge 2) begins, all remaining unused tickets from the previous cycle (Charge 1) must be counted as expired instead of being carried over into active use. Furthermore, Charge 2 must still appear as a row in the monthly CSV report if it has started within the target month, even if no lessons were taken.

[Precondition]

* **Plan:** Zipan Monthly-5 plan (5 lessons/month).
* **Charge 1 Timeline:** 11/30 ~ 12/29 (Ticket expiry: 12/29 based on contract period limit).
* **Charge 2 Timeline:** Opens on 12/30 (No lessons taken thereafter).
* **Rule:** Ticket validity = contract period. Remaining tickets must flash to expired at the contract break date (12/30).

[Steps]

1. Open Charge 1 for the monthly-5 plan on 11/30.
2. Record 1 lesson taken on 11/30 (Charge 1, remaining=4).
3. Record 1 lesson taken on 12/1.
4. Open Charge 2 on 12/30; record 0 lessons taken thereafter.
5. Generate the November monthly report (target_ym=202511) via the 202512 batch.
6. Generate the December monthly report (target_ym=202512) via the 202601 batch.

[Expected]

**① CSV for 202511 (generated by 202512 batch)**
target_ym,total,carried_over,taken,expired,remaining,paid_price,calculation_basis,notes
202511,5,0,1,0,4,(unit)×1,unit×(1+0),"Charge 1: Nov portion. remaining=4 (valid until 12/30)."

**② CSV for 202512 (generated by 202601 batch)**
target_ym,total,carried_over,taken,expired,remaining,paid_price,calculation_basis,notes
202512,5,4,1,3,0,(unit)×4,unit×(1+3),"Charge 1: ★ carried_over=4. taken=1. expired=3 (remaining tickets expire at 12/30 when Charge 2 opens due to no-carryover rule), remaining=0."
202512,5,0,0,0,5,0,unit×0,"Charge 2: No lessons; charge started → must appear in CSV. remaining=5, paid_price=0."

[Acceptance Criteria]

* Zipan reporting must enforce a hard-stop expiration rule at the contract end date; remaining unspent lessons cannot carry over into active availability for the next period.
* Active charges that have started within the target month must always generate a report row, ensuring that zero-usage rows preserve their baseline `total` as `remaining` with a `paid_price` of 0.
