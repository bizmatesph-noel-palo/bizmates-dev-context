# [ASC-211]
B2B Order 10021998 — Last Charge Expiration Failure (60-Day Validity Bug)

[Story]
Even though `charge_id 2880533` is the final charge item under order #10021998, it is not expiring. The system incorrectly carries over the lessons to the next month instead of expiring them at the end of the order term. This follows the same pattern where last charges are incorrectly treated as having an extended 60-day validity regardless of contract closure.

[Precondition]
Student 181524 has the following sequential charges under Order 10021998 (Plan: 8 lessons):

* **Charge 1 (2880531):** 2026-01-11 to 2026-02-10 | paid_price: 10560 | First charge
* **Charge 2 (2880532):** 2026-02-11 to 2026-03-10 | paid_price: 10560 | Second charge
* **Charge 3 (2880533):** 2026-03-11 to 2026-04-10 | paid_price: 10560 | Last charge of order string

[Steps]
Generate the April monthly report (target_ym=202604) via the 202605 batch execution cycle.

```csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202604,181524,11884,10021998,2880532,8,3,1,2,0,3960
```
