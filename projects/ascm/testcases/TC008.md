Case 9c - Cooling-Off — Lessons Taken, Cross-Month Execution

[Story]
When a student executes a cooling-off within 8 days of plan start but the execution crosses into the following month, the normal charge row appears in the plan start month with lessons taken and remaining tickets carried over. In the execution month, a second normal charge row appears with all carried-over tickets expired, plus a CF refund charge row with a negative paid_price. The refund target_ym is the month of CF execution.

How to Verify:
[Precondition] Student [student_id] has the following charges (plan: 15 lessons/month at ¥980/ticket = ¥14,700/month):
order_no,charge_id,type,start_date,end_date,usage_notes
[order_no],[charge_1_id],B2C,2026-01-28,2026-02-27,"Charge 1. 3 lessons taken 1/28–1/31. 12 tickets remaining at month-end → carried over to Feb."
[order_no],[cf_refund_id],CF Refund,2026-02-05,2026-02-05,"CF executed 2/5 (within 8 days of 1/28 start). 12 carried-over tickets expired. 12 × ¥980 = ¥11,760 negative. Full plan ¥14,700 negative."

[Steps]
Student takes 3 lessons between 1/28–1/31 (12 tickets remaining at month-end)
Cooling-off is executed on 2/5 (within 8 days of 1/28 plan start) → cross-month execution in February
All 12 carried-over tickets become expired in February
Generate the January monthly report (target_ym=202601) via the 202602 batch
Generate the February monthly report (target_ym=202602) via the 202603 batch
