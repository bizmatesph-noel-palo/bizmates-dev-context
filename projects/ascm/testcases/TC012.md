[ASC-157]
Case 2 - Charge 2 Must Appear in CSV — Expired Timing Based on Ticket Expiry Date (60-Day Validity)

[Story]
When a student starts Charge 2 under the same order_no but takes no lessons, Charge 2 must still appear in the Freee batch CSV with remaining=8 and paid_price=0. Additionally, Charge 1's remaining tickets must expire in the correct month based on the 60-day ticket expiry date — not the charge end date.

[Precondition]
Student [student_id] has the following charges (plan: 8 lessons/month, non-FLP):
order_no,charge_id,start_date,end_date,ticket_expiry,usage_notes
[order_no],[charge_1_id],2026-01-01,2026-01-31,2026-02-28,"Charge 1. 5 lessons taken 1/1–1/31. Ticket expiry: 2/28 (60 days from 1/31)."
[order_no],[charge_2_id],2026-02-01,2026-02-28,2026-03-31,"Charge 2. Same order_no. No lessons taken in February."

8 lessons/month

[Steps]
Student takes 5 lessons between 1/1–1/31 (Charge 1, remaining=3)
Charge 2 opens on 2/1 (same order_no); no lessons taken in February
Generate the January monthly report (target_ym=202601) via the 202602 batch
Generate the February monthly report (target_ym=202602) via the 202603 batch

[Expected]
File1: 202601_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260201).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],8,0,5,0,3,6600
Charge 1: taken=5, remaining=3 (tickets valid until 2/28 — 60-day expiry), paid_price=6600 (¥1,320 × 5).

File2: 202602_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260301).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202602,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],8,3,0,3,0,3960
202602,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_2_id],8,0,0,0,8,0

Charge 1:
carried_over=3 (from Jan remaining), taken=0, expired=3 (60-day expiry 2/28 reached in February — expiry is triggered by ticket expiry date, NOT by charge end_date), remaining=0, paid_price=3960 (¥1,320 × (0+3)).

Charge 2:
Charge started on 2/1 but no lessons taken. Must still appear in CSV. remaining=8, paid_price=0.

[Acceptance Criteria]
Even if a student took no lessons, Charge 2 must appear in the CSV if it has started within the target month.
Charge 1 expired tickets must be attributed to the month the 60-day ticket expiry date falls in — not the month of the charge end_date.
