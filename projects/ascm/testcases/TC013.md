[ASC-157]
Case 3 - B2B REST — Remaining Tickets Must Expire When Contract Ends (No Renewal)

[Story]
When a B2B student's contract expires and is not renewed, the student enters REST status. All remaining tickets at the contract end date must be counted as expired in the final charge's monthly report row. There is no Charge 2 since the contract was not renewed.

[Precondition]
Student [student_id] has the following charges (plan: 8 lessons/month, B2B):
order_no,charge_id,start_date,end_date,ticket_expiry,usage_notes
[order_no],[charge_1_id],2026-01-31,2026-03-01,2026-03-31,"Charge 1. 1 lesson on 1/31, 5 lessons 2/1–3/1. Contract expires 3/1. No renewal → REST on 3/2. Remaining 2 tickets expire."

[Steps]
Student takes 1 lesson on 1/31 (Charge 1)
Student takes 5 lessons between 2/1–3/1 (Charge 1, total taken=6, remaining=2)
Contract expires on 3/1. Student does not renew → enters REST status on 3/2
No Charge 2 is issued
Generate the January monthly report (target_ym=202601) via the 202602 batch
Generate the February monthly report (target_ym=202602) via the 202603 batch

[Expected]

File1: 202601_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260201).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],8,0,1,0,7,1320

File1_Note:
Charge 1 (Jan): taken=1 (lesson on 1/31), remaining=7, paid_price=1320 (¥1,320 × 1).

File2: 202602_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260301).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202602,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],8,7,5,2,0,9240

File2_Note1:
Charge 1 (Feb): carried_over=7 (from Jan remaining), taken=5, expired=2 (remaining 2 tickets expire when contract ends on 3/1 — B2B REST, no renewal), remaining=0, paid_price=9240 (¥1,320 × (5+2)).

File2_Note2:
No Charge 2 row — contract was not renewed.

[Acceptance Criteria]
When a B2B contract expires without renewal, all remaining tickets must be counted as expired in the final charge row of that month's CSV.
No carry-over should occur after contract expiry under REST status.
