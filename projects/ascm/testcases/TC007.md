[ASC-151]
Case 9b - Cooling-Off — No Lessons Taken, Same-Month Execution

[Story]
When a student executes a cooling-off within 8 days of plan start and has taken zero lessons, the normal charge row must NOT appear. Only the CF refund charge row with the full plan amount as a negative paid_price must appear in the CSV.

How to Verify:
[Precondition] Student [student_id] has the following charges (plan: 15 lessons/month at ¥980/ticket = ¥14,700/month):
order_no,charge_id,type,start_date,end_date,usage_notes
[order_no],[charge_1_id],B2C,2026-01-01,2026-01-31,"Charge 1. No lessons taken. Cooling-off executed 1/8 (within 8 days). All 15 tickets subject to refund."
[order_no],[cf_refund_id],CF Refund,2026-01-08,2026-01-08,"CF refund. Full plan amount ¥14,700 negative."

[Steps]
No lessons taken after plan start on 1/1
Cooling-off is executed on 1/8 (within 8 days of plan start) → all 15 tickets subject to refund
Generate the January monthly report (target_ym=202601) via the 202602 batch

[Expected]

File1: 202601_03_MonthlyRateCalculation(20260201).csv
target_ym,student_id,department_id,order_no,charge_id,total,number_of_carried_over_lessons,number_of_lessons_taken,number_of_expired_lessons,number_of_remaining_lessons,paid_price
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[charge_1_id],15,0,0,15,0,14700
202601,[student_id],[dept_id],[order_no],[cf_refund_id],,,,,,,-14700

Charge 1 (normal):
taken=0, expired=15 (all tickets voided by cooling-off), remaining=0, paid_price=14700 (¥980 × 15).

CF refund charge:
paid_price=-14700 (full plan amount negative). total, carried_over, taken, expired, remaining left blank.

Normal charge row does NOT appear independently — only the CF refund row represents the refund.
The normal row is output to account for the expired tickets.

Both rows appear in target_ym=202601 because cooling-off was executed in January.
